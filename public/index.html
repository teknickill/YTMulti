MultiStream

Watch several live streams at once

Search for a stream by name — or paste any of these, one at a time or comma-separated:

  • @handle or a channel link — follows whatever that channel is streaming, and picks up the next stream when it goes live
  • A live or video link: youtube.com/watch?v=…, youtu.be/…, youtube.com/live/…
  • An 11-character video ID

/Jump to the search / Add box

Tips

  • Click a stream to give it the sound. Its buttons appear while you move the mouse over it (on phones: tap it). Drag a stream by its name to reorder (on phones: ⋯ → Move up / down).
  • 💬 on a stream opens its chat in a full-height column on the right (under the video on phones). Two chats stack; three or more become tabs. Drag the column's edge to resize it; ↗ pops a chat into its own window.
  • In Focus layout, ⤢ on a stream makes it the big one.
  • Browsers block sound until you click the page once — the app reminds you.
  • Some streams can't be embedded (the uploader turned it off). Those show an Open on YouTube button.
  • Saved sets (🔖): name the streams on screen and switch between sets in one tap. The sleep timer is there too: it pauses everything when time's up.
  • Recent: tap the empty search box to re-add channels you watched before.
  • When a channel you follow goes live, a message pops up — tap Listen to give it the sound.
  • Data use: Full plays everything normally. Saver drops every stream to low quality (the one with sound a bit higher) and stops the others while the tab is in the background. Min plays only the stream with sound; the rest show a picture that updates each minute — click one to switch to it.
